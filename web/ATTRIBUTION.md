# Third-party art attribution

Dungeon Debt ships with art from three sources:

1. **Unit portraits** (all hero and enemy art in `web/assets/heroes/` and `web/assets/enemies/`) — derived from the **Kenney Tiny Dungeon** pack, released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**.
2. **Combat effect sprites** (`web/assets/effects/`) — icons from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net) under the **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)** license.
3. **Animated 3D combat models** (`web/assets/models/`) — first-party, procedurally generated by `gen_unit_models.cjs` and released **CC0 1.0** with the rest of the project's original art.

License floor for this project (locked R005-1): **CC0 and CC BY** only. CC BY-SA (copyleft) and any Non-Commercial (NC) variant are explicitly rejected so the game stays free to ship as a paid commercial release.

## Animated 3D combat models (`web/assets/models/`) — first-party CC0

Every combat unit (one `<unitId>.gltf` per hero and per enemy, plus `hero-fallback.gltf`
and `enemy-fallback.gltf`) is an original, low-poly animated model **authored by this
project** — generated procedurally by `gen_unit_models.cjs` from the unit roster in
`DataRepository`. Each model is a recognizable, role-appropriate figure built from a
shared unit-cube mesh and ships four node-animation clips: `idle`, `attack`, `hit`,
`death`.

Units are mapped to a small set of **archetype silhouettes**, varied per id by material
tint and swappable props rather than authoring 82 bespoke meshes:

- **mage** (wizard/sorcerer/warlock/apprentice/enchanter + caster-flavored economy
  enemies) — robed figure with a pointed hat and a staff topped by an orb
- **support** (priest/cleric/druid/bard/healer/medic/treasurer) — robed caster with a
  holy staff
- **archer** (ranger/frugal_archer/dungeon_archer) — figure holding a drawn bow + arrow
- **knight** (knight/paladin/squire/guards/grunts/protectors/tanks) — helmeted, with a
  breastplate, shield, and sword
- **heavy** (warrior/barbarian/fighter/brutes/brawlers) — bulky melee figure with an axe
- **rogue** (ninja/rogue/goblin_thief) — hooded figure with a dagger
- **golem** — oversized stone figure with fists
- **blob** (slime/leech/fiend/dummy) — rounded body with eyes
- **flyer** (bats/imps/wraiths) — winged body with flapping idle
- **bosses** (mintmaster, banker-king, infernal_auditor, dungeon_champion, hoard_fiend)
  — their base archetype enlarged and given a distinct silhouette (gold crown + cape)

Because these are first-party works, they are released **CC0 1.0** (public domain),
consistent with the project license floor. No third-party model packs are bundled; the
Quaternius / KayKit / Kenney 3D packs named in the design handoff were evaluated but
**not** included — the procedural first-party approach was kept to honor the CC0 floor
without fabricating attribution and to keep `web/assets/models/` small (~6–9 KB per
file, ~0.9 MB total). To swap in external CC0/CC BY models later, drop replacement
`<unitId>.gltf`/`.glb` files into `web/assets/models/` (matching the ids in
`web/src/ui/board/GeneratedModelIds.js`) and record their source + license here.

### Vendored runtime loader (MIT)

The combat board loads these models with the **three.js** glTF loader, vendored under
`web/vendor/`:

- `GLTFLoader.js`, `BufferGeometryUtils.js`, `SkeletonUtils.js` — three.js examples
  addons (r184), © three.js authors, **MIT license** (<https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js>).
  Import paths were rewritten to load the project's local `three.module.js`; otherwise
  unmodified. MIT is permissive and compatible with the project license floor.
